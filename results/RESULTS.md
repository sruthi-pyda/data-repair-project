# Results — risk-aware LLM-assisted data repair

Generated by `python run_experiment.py --all --splits 20 --seed 42` on full
datasets (no cap), mock repair mode. Every number here regenerates from that
one command; nothing is hand-edited. Tables in `results/tables/`, figures in
`results/figures/`, per-cell predictions in `results/predictions/`, provenance
in `results/run_metadata.json`.

Costs are abstract **experimental penalty units** from `configs/costs.yaml`.
Not money, not clinical harm, not supplied by the REIN benchmark.

## 0. Read this first — the soft-leakage result (W5.1b / W5.2b)

Every detection and repair number in this document is produced with
`DatasetProfile` built from `clean_df`, so the detector's IQR fences, valid
ranges and vocabularies — and the repairer's imputation medians — are derived
from clean aggregate statistics. That has now been ablated
(`results/w5_leakage_ablation.md`, `results/w5_raha_leakage_check.md`).

**The headline result in §1 survives: zero worst-case regret holds under both
clean and dirty profiling.** The decision layer is what this project
contributes, and it does not depend on the leakage.

**The detection numbers do not survive.** breast_cancer PR-AUC falls
0.6712 → 0.5224 with disjoint CIs and its repair top-1 falls 0.673 → 0.506;
hospital goes to *zero* flags; rayyan and beers collapse to F1 0.0558. adult
and smartfactory are unaffected, for mechanical reasons given in `CLAUDE.md`
§3. Consequently our reported win over Raha reverses on a zero-knowledge
basis — see `results/w5_raha_comparison.md`, whose conclusion is corrected at
the top.

### And on adult, the system equals doing nothing (W7.0c)

From `results/w5_complete_costs.md`, total harm including undetected
corruption:

| policy | total harm | vs do-nothing |
|---|---:|---:|
| **proposed (calibrated risk-aware)** | **8,505** | **0** |
| always-abstain | 8,505 | 0 |
| _do-nothing (no detector)_ | 8,505 | 0 |
| always-human | 20,163 | +11,658 |
| always-auto | 66,795 | +58,290 |

The proposed policy ties *do-nothing* on adult. It beats every active
baseline by a wide margin — avoiding 11,658 to 58,290 units of avoidable
harm — but it adds **nothing** over having no detector at all, because
adult's 1,701 genuine corruptions are never detected and so enter every
policy's cost identically.

That is the honest reading of adult's "0" wherever it appears in §1, and it
should be stated in those words rather than left as a cell in a table. It is
a real property of the method on valid-to-valid corruption, not a reporting
artefact.

So: cite §1 freely for the *decision* claim, which survives both arms.

## 1. The headline result

No fixed policy is good on all three datasets. The adaptive one is.

| policy | adult | breast_cancer | smartfactory | **worst-case regret** |
|---|---:|---:|---:|---:|
| **proposed (calibrated + cost-aware)** | **0** | **1,615** | **14,186** | **0** |
| always human review | 11,658 | 1,775 | 14,186 | 11,658 |
| raw confidence ≥ 0.70 | 11,658 | 1,720 | 14,186 | 11,658 |
| raw confidence ≥ 0.80 | 11,658 | 1,770 | 14,186 | 11,658 |
| always abstain | 0 | 3,550 | 35,465 | 21,279 |
| raw confidence ≥ 0.60 | 56,418 | 3,780 | 39,785 | 56,418 |
| always auto-repair | 58,290 | 5,800 | 126,275 | 112,089 |

Regret = how much worse than the best policy *for that dataset*. The proposed
method attains the best cost on **every** dataset, so its worst-case regret is
zero. Every fixed policy is beaten badly somewhere: always-human and the
threshold baselines waste 11,658 units reviewing Adult's false alarms;
always-abstain is cheapest on Adult but abandons real errors on the other two.

This is the claim the project set out to test. A fixed rule cannot know that
Adult's flags are false alarms while Smart Factory's are real; the calibrated
policy infers it per cell and acts accordingly.

### Per-dataset, stated plainly

- **breast_cancer — wins.** 1,615 vs 1,720 for the best baseline (−6.1 %).
  Over 20 calibration splits: 1,631.50 [1,620.75, 1,649.00], still clearly
  below the baseline.
- **adult — ties always-abstain at 0,** and beats everything else by 11,658.
  The tie is not a null result: always-abstain achieves 0 here by never acting,
  which costs it 21,279 elsewhere. The proposed method *derived* inaction from
  the data; the baseline was handed it.
- **smartfactory — ties always-human at 14,186.** No improvement. At the
  shipped default the detector raises no false alarms, so abstention has
  nothing to do, and no repair is confident enough to auto-apply (best
  p = 0.58 against a 0.92 break-even). The policy correctly reduces to "review
  everything". **Reported as a tie, not spun as a win.**

## 2. Calibration, with confidence intervals (20 splits, 95 % bootstrap)

This is the first time these numbers carry error bars, and it changes which
claims survive.

| dataset | method | Brier | ECE | splits fitted |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| breast_cancer | raw | 0.1982 [0.1936, 0.2029] | 0.1074 [0.0973, 0.1177] | 20/20 |
| breast_cancer | logistic | 0.1979 [0.1920, 0.2040] | 0.1299 [0.1177, 0.1414] | 20/20 |
| breast_cancer | **isotonic** | **0.1842 [0.1787, 0.1900]** | **0.0652 [0.0527, 0.0787]** | 20/20 |
| breast_cancer | multivariate | 0.1968 [0.1910, 0.2029] | 0.0846 [0.0733, 0.0964] | 20/20 |
| smartfactory | raw | 0.2732 [0.2727, 0.2737] | 0.2925 [0.2901, 0.2949] | 20/20 |
| smartfactory | logistic | 0.1691 [0.1674, 0.1708] | 0.0577 [0.0556, 0.0601] | 20/20 |
| smartfactory | **isotonic** | **0.1632 [0.1615, 0.1649]** | **0.0127 [0.0096, 0.0158]** | 20/20 |
| smartfactory | multivariate | 0.1883 [0.1872, 0.1894] | 0.1556 [0.1519, 0.1595] | 20/20 |
| adult | raw | 0.4141 [0.4139, 0.4144] | 0.6429 [0.6427, 0.6431] | 20/20 |
| adult | logistic / isotonic / multivariate | — | — | **0/20 ⚠** |

**What survives resampling**

- **Isotonic beats raw on both fittable datasets, decisively.** The intervals
  do not overlap on either. This is the result to report.
- **Adult cannot be calibrated at all** — no method fitted in any of 20 splits.
  Printed as `—`, never as a number.

**What does not survive, including one of our own contributions**

- **Logistic vs raw on breast_cancer is nothing.** 0.1979 vs 0.1982, intervals
  almost coincident. The single-split improvement previously quoted in
  `CLAUDE.md` §4 was noise. It is now removed.
- **The multivariate calibrator (the §5.3 contribution) does not pay off.**
  On breast_cancer it improves ECE (0.0846 vs raw 0.1074, non-overlapping) but
  its Brier is indistinguishable from raw. On smartfactory it is **worse than
  both logistic and isotonic on both metrics** (Brier 0.1883 vs 0.1632; ECE
  0.1556 vs 0.0127). Adding self-consistency and entropy as calibrator features
  is, on this evidence, **not an improvement** — it should be reported as a
  negative ablation result, not as a contribution.

## 3. Repair quality and detection (T1)

| dataset | detections | precision | recall | F1 | top-1 repair accuracy | genuine corruptions | false negatives |
|---|---:|---:|---:|---:|---:|---:|---:|
| adult | 5,829 | 0.000 | 0.000 | 0.000 | 0.000 | 1,701 | **1,701** |
| breast_cancer | 392 | 1.000 | 0.563 | 0.720 | 0.673 | 631 | 276 |
| smartfactory | 7,093 | 1.000 | 1.000 | 1.000 | 0.288 | 7,093 | 0 |

Two failure modes, both worth stating:

- **Adult: perfect failure of detection.** Every flag is a false positive and
  every real corruption is missed. The decision layer handles this correctly
  (it abstains on all of them) but cannot repair what was never found.
- **Smart Factory: perfect detection, poor repair.** Every corrupted cell is
  found, but the mock repairer gets only 28.8 % right, which is exactly why no
  cell clears the auto-repair bar.

## 4. What these numbers do NOT show

- **Not an LLM result.** Every repair came from `src/repair/mock_repair.py`.
  Phase 12 is not implemented, so these numbers bound the **decision layer**,
  not an LLM's repair quality. The title's "LLM-Assisted" is not yet earned.
- **False negatives are priced at zero.** 1,701 undetected corruptions on
  Adult, 276 on breast_cancer. No policy is charged for them, so every cost
  here describes **the flagged cells only**.
- **Adult's 0 means zero residual-corruption cost on the flagged cells** — not
  "zero cost", not "the data is clean".
- **Abstention does not appear at shipped defaults** on breast_cancer or
  Smart Factory, because at those settings the detector raises no false alarms.
  It is correct for abstention to be unused there. The configurations where all
  three actions occur (breast_cancer at Tukey fence k=1.5; Smart Factory with
  outlier flagging on) are in `results/tables/three_actions_ablation.md`.
- **Per cell, only two of the three actions are ever live** (the human/abstain
  boundary is horizontal in q). All three occur across a population that mixes
  real errors with false alarms. See `docs/cost_semantics.md` §4.
- **Soft leakage is unchanged.** `DatasetProfile` is still built from
  `clean_df`, so detector fences and imputation statistics carry
  population-level clean knowledge in every policy alike.
- **One cost configuration per dataset.** The costs are assumptions; the
  sensitivity of these rankings to them has not been swept here.

## 5. Reproducing

```bash
python run_experiment.py --all --splits 20 --seed 42    # ~11 minutes
python run_experiment.py --dataset breast_cancer        # one dataset
python run_experiment.py --quick --all                  # fast, NOT paper-grade
```

Deterministic: re-running with the same flags produces byte-identical tables
and predictions. `results/run_metadata.json` records the git commit, seeds,
split protocol, library versions and per-dataset counts.
