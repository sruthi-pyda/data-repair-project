# W12 — cost sensitivity and the calibration ablation

Generated by `experiments/w12_policy_ablation.py`. Seed 42, full
datasets. Both experiments are arithmetic over quantities the pipeline
already produced, so no detector is refitted and nothing here depends
on a new model.

All policies are scored on **total harm**, which charges wrong
auto-repairs, human reviews, and every corrupted cell left unrepaired
**including corruption the detector never flagged**. That last term is
what stops a policy that detects nothing from looking good, and
`do nothing` is carried as a reference line for the same reason.

## 1. Setup

| dataset | decided cells | corruptions missed entirely | total corruptions |
|---|---:|---:|---:|
| breast_cancer | 355 | 169 | 524 |
| smartfactory | 7,093 | 0 | 7,093 |
| adult | 5,829 | 1,701 | 1,701 |

The cost grid fixes `C_human = 1` and sweeps `C_wrong` over [2.0, 5.0, 10.0, 25.0, 50.0] and `C_abstain` over [0.5, 1.0, 2.0, 5.0, 10.0], giving **25 configurations per dataset** against the single configuration used previously.

## 2. Cost sensitivity — is zero regret an artefact of one configuration?

How often each policy is best or tied-best on total harm, out of
25 cost configurations per dataset.

| policy | breast_cancer | smartfactory | adult | all |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| **proposed (calibrated)** | 24/25 | 25/25 | 25/25 | **74/75** |
| uncalibrated (raw conf.) | 0/25 | 21/25 | 25/25 | 46/75 |
| always repair | 1/25 | 0/25 | 0/25 | 1/75 |
| always review | 8/25 | 16/25 | 0/25 | 24/75 |
| always abstain | 5/25 | 9/25 | 25/25 | 39/75 |
| conf >= 0.70 | 0/25 | 16/25 | 0/25 | 16/75 |
| conf >= 0.90 | 0/25 | 16/25 | 0/25 | 16/75 |

A policy counted here is best or tied on that configuration; several
can tie at once, so columns need not sum to the number of
configurations.

## 3. Calibration ablation — does calibrating `p` change decisions?

The identical cost rule, driven by **raw confidence** instead of the
calibrated `p`. Everything else is held fixed, so any difference is
attributable to calibration alone.

| outcome | configurations |
|---|---:|
| calibrated strictly better | 29/75 |
| uncalibrated strictly better | 0/75 |
| identical | 46/75 |

Mean harm saved by calibrating: **183.0** units (median 0.0, range 0.0 to 3,188.0).
