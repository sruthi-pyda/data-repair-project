# W10 — does stratified threshold selection convert the hospital loss?

Generated by `experiments/w10_threshold.py`. Planned in
`docs/w10_plan.md`, committed before the code. Full datasets, budget
20, 10 label draws, seeds 42..51. Every figure is
**mean [min, max]** over draws.

## 0. What is and is not being changed

The detector is **identical** to W9.3's and zero clean knowledge:
conditional detector with `profile=None`, plus the gated text fallback
on columns it cannot model, unioned with the rule detector built from
the dirty frame in both positions. **Only the operating point moves.**
No detection number changes.

`results/w9c_raha_rematch.md` split our three losses in two:
**hospital's oracle bound exceeds Raha's F1**, so its detector is
already good enough and the threshold is what loses. beers and rayyan
lose even at their oracles, so they are genuine detection deficits —
included here only to check this change does not make them worse.

## 5. Caveats

- This is **threshold selection, not detection**. beers and rayyan lose
  to Raha at their oracle bounds, so no operating point can convert
  them; that needs a better detector.
- Raha ran at one budget and one seed per dataset, and its
  `breast_cancer` result remains anomalous (13 flags of 8,388) and is
  still not quoted as evidence of anything.
- Detection only. Raha makes no repair, review or abstain decision.
