# W8-B — learned-policy synthetic supervision

Generated by `experiments/w8b_synth.py`. Planned in `docs/w8_plan.md`
§4 (W8-B), committed before the code. Seed 42, row cap 8000,
label budget **20 cells**.

## 0. The arms, and the circularity they test

Learning a corruption policy requires knowing which cells are
corrupt, which is the problem being solved. The arms make that
circularity measurable rather than arguable.

| arm | policy | deployable | note |
|---|---|---|---|
| `a0` | designed policy | yes | zero labels |
| `a1_rand` | learned policy, random budget | yes | 20 labels |
| `a1_top` | learned policy, top-score budget | yes | 20 labels |
| `a1b_bal` | learned policy, balanced budget | **no** | SEMI-ORACLE (20 labels, half forced positive) |
| `a2` | oracle policy | **no** | ORACLE — all labels |

`a1b_bal` and `a2` are **not methods**. `a1b_bal` needs the labels to
choose a balanced sample, and `a2` reads every label; both exist to
separate *budget placement* from *policy estimation* from *classifier
capacity*.

The budgeted label is HoloDetect-style — "this cell is wrong and the
right value is v" — which is **stronger than Raha's binary flag**. A
transition distribution is not estimable without the correct value, so
the arm-1 numbers below are an *upper* bound on what the same budget of
binary labels would give.

recomputed on exactly the columns scored here — never carried over from

## 7. Condition 5 — not just coverage

A column enters every table above only when the unsupervised
conditional detector produced at least 3 distinct scores in it. The
rule is arm-independent, so the baseline and every arm are scored on an
identical column population — the W6.1 pre-fix bug was exactly this.

| dataset | columns | scored | excluded |
|---|---:|---:|---|
| adult | 15 | 15 | — |
| breast_cancer | 12 | 10 | `Unnamed: 0`, `Sample code number` |
| smartfactory | 19 | 19 | — |
| soccer | 10 | 7 | `name`, `surname`, `birthplace` |
| tax | 15 | 10 | `f_name`, `l_name`, `phone`, `city`, `state` |

## 8. Condition 7 — no leakage

Asserted in code, not claimed here: `tests/test_synth_supervised.py`
`TestNoLeakage` runs arm 0 end to end with both clean-reading entry
points monkeypatched to raise, and again with the clean frame replaced
by a tripwire that raises on any column access. The detector's
`__init__`, `fit` and `score_cells` signatures are scanned for any
parameter naming clean data. This script fits arm 0 before it reads any
clean data and asserts that ordering.
