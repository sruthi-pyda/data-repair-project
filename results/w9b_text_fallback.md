# W9.2b — does the text fallback detector earn its place?

Generated by `experiments/w9b_text_fallback.py`. Specified in
`docs/w9_plan.md` §W9.2b with six requirements fixed before the run.
5 row splits of up to 4,000 rows, seeds 42..46. Every figure is
**mean [min, max] over splits** — no single split is reported alone
(requirement 6).

## 0. The design decision that matters most

A fallback that covers new columns changes **which cells are
evaluated**, and that alone can manufacture an apparent improvement.
So three populations are kept strictly apart:

- **shared** — columns both detectors score. The only like-for-like
  comparison, on identical cells.
- **new** — columns only the fallback scores, judged against the
  prevalence of those cells.
- **whole table** — combined system vs conditional-only over an
  identical cell universe.

Combination is **conditional-priority**, so where the conditional
detector covers a column its score is used unchanged. On a dataset it
already covers fully the combined system is *identical* to
conditional-only, making requirement 4 hold by construction.
